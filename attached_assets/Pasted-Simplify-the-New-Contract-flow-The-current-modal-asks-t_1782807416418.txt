- Rental Contract

BUSINESS RULE

In Job Flow, a contract means recurring work or recurring supply for a client.

A contract can include:
- labour/service
- physical rental/product items
- both service and rental/product items together

Do not force the user to choose Service Contract or Rental Contract at the start.

Example:
A sanitary bin contract includes:
- placement of the bin at the client site
- regular cleaning/service of the bin
- replacement of the bin if it is too dirty to clean on site

This is both a physical item and a recurring service.

Therefore, the contract form must support combined contract lines.

NEW CONTRACT BUTTON

When the user clicks:

+ New Contract

Do not show the Service Contract / Rental Contract choice modal.

Instead, open the New Contract form directly.
