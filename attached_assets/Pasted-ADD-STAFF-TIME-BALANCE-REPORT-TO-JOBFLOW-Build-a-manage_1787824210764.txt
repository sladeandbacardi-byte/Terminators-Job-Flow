ADD STAFF TIME BALANCE REPORT TO JOBFLOW

Build a management report that combines:

- Approved Overtime
- Approved Authorised Time Off

for each employee and calculates a clear net time balance.

Do NOT replace the existing Overtime or Time Off records.

This report must use the same underlying approved records already stored in JobFlow.

--------------------------------------------------
1. REPORT NAME
--------------------------------------------------

Add:

Operations > Time & Overtime > Time Balance Report

Also allow access from:

Reports > Staff Time Balance

--------------------------------------------------
2. CALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------

For each employee calculate:

NET TIME BALANCE =
APPROVED OVERTIME MINUTES
minus
APPROVED AUTHORISED TIME OFF MINUTES

Example:

Overtime:
12 hrs 30 min

Authorised Time Off:
7 hrs

Net Time Balance:
+5 hrs 30 min

Another example:

Overtime:
3 hrs

Authorised Time Off:
6 hrs 30 min

Net Time Balance:
-3 hrs 30 min

Store/calculate using MINUTES internally.

Do not use decimal hours.

--------------------------------------------------
3. IMPORTANT RULE
--------------------------------------------------

ONLY APPROVED records must affect the Net Time Balance.

Pending Overtime:
DO NOT include

Pending Time Off:
DO NOT include

Rejected records:
DO NOT include

Show pending amounts separately for information.

13. PAYROLL CAUTION
--------------------------------------------------

The Time Balance Report is a MANAGEMENT INFORMATION report.

Do NOT automatically alter payroll calculations based on the Net Time Balance.

Keep these values separate:

Approved Overtime
Approved Authorised Time Off
Net Time Balance

Management can decide how the information is used for payroll.

14. MONTHLY BALANCE / CARRY FORWARD
--------------------------------------------------

Add an optional:

CARRY FORWARD BALANCE

Do not enable automatic carry-forward by default yet.

Show:

Opening Balance
Period Overtime
Period Time Off
Closing Balance

Formula:

Closing Balance =
Opening Balance
+ Approved Overtime
- Approved Time Off

Example:

Opening Balance:
+2 hrs 30 min

August Overtime:
+8 hrs

August Time Off:
-4 hrs

Closing Balance:
+6 hrs 30 min

For now create the structure and display, but do not automatically carry balances into the next month unless Admin enables it later.

--------------------------------------------------
17. PERMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Normal employees:

May view their own Time Balance only if enabled.

They must not be able to:
- edit calculated balances
- view other employees
- approve entries

Admin/Management:

May view all employee balances.
May filter/export.
May open underlying records.
May approve/reject/reopen through the existing Time & Overtime system.

--------------------------------------------------
18. DO NOT DUPLICATE DATA
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT create a separate database of overtime and time-off values just for this report.

Calculate the report from the existing approved time records.

The report must always reflect the actual underlying records.

If an entry is approved, rejected, reopened or edited, the report must update accordingly.

20. IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------

This feature should extend the existing:

Operations > Time & Overtime

system.

Do not build another disconnected HR module.

The goal is to give management one clear answer for every employee:

How much approved overtime have they worked?
How much authorised time off have they received?
What is their current net time balance?
