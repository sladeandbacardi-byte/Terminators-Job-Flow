FIX TIME & OVERTIME ADMIN VIEW

Under:

Operations > Time & Overtime

this page must be the Admin/Management view for ALL employee time entries.

It must display both:

- Overtime
- Authorised Time Off

Do not make Admin search individual employee profiles to find these records.

--------------------------------------------------

Pending Overtime
Pending Time Off
Approved Overtime This Month
Approved Time Off This Month

Then add tabs:

ALL
OVERTIME

PENDING APPROVAL
APPROVED
REJECTED

--------------------------------------------------
MY TIME
--------------------------------------------------

Keep Operations > My Time as the logged-in user's PERSONAL view only.

My Time must show only the current user's:

- Overtime
- Authorised Time Off
- Monthly totals
- Recent entries

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------

The distinction must be:

MY TIME = personal employee view

TIME & OVERTIME = management/admin view for all staff

Do not duplicate or split the records into separate databases.
Both views must use the same underlying time records.
