MAKE JOB FLOW HOLISTIC AND CONNECTED FROM LEADS TO INVOICES

I want Job Flow to work as one connected business system.

Everything must flow into each other:

Lead → Quote → Accepted Work → Job / Contract → Schedule → Field Diary → Invoice → Payment → Reports

The client must be the centre of the system.

If I open a client information panel, I must be able to see the full history of that client in one place.

CLIENT MASTER RECORD

Create one central client master record.

Every client must have one main client profile.

Client details must include:

* Client ID
* Company name
* Trading name, optional
* Contact person
* Phone
* Email
* VAT number, optional
* Company registration number, optional
* Billing address
* Site address or multiple site addresses
* Preferred contact method
* Account status
* Credit limit, optional
* Payment terms
* Assigned sales rep
* Notes
* Active / inactive status

Avoid duplicate clients.

If a lead, quote, job, contract or invoice is created for an existing client, it must link to the existing client record.

PERMISSION RULES

Managing Member:
Can view full client history.

Sales:
Can view client leads, quotes, contacts and sales notes.
Can see job/invoice status summary but not sensitive finance details unless permitted.

Service:
Can view client service history, jobs, field diaries and site details.
Can see invoice status summary if needed.

Finance:
Can view invoices, payments, debtors and legal entity data.
Can view linked quote/job details needed for billing.

Technician:
Can only view assigned jobs, site details and relevant field diary history.
No finance access.
