# 3. PAGE PURPOSE

This must NOT look like a normal accounting report.

It should look like an **owner's business cockpit**.

The page should answer these questions immediately:

1. How much cash is Terminators generating?
2. Where are we losing or failing to collect revenue?
3. How much is still owed to Jan?
4. How quickly are we paying Jan off?
5. How much extra have we paid?
6. When will Jan be fully paid at the current payment rate?
7. How much capital have we accumulated for property?
8. Are sales and profit growing?
9. How much additional monthly cash has been created?
10. How much money could potentially be recovered from revenue leakage?

Keep the design simple, visual and executive-level.

# 26. MOST IMPORTANT BUSINESS PRINCIPLE

This dashboard is intended to help the owner convert:

**better operations**
↓
**recovered revenue**
↓
**higher recurring income**
↓
**higher free cash generation**
↓
**faster Jan repayment**
↓
**property investment capital**

Therefore the page should emphasise CASH GENERATION and CAPITAL PROGRESS rather than simply celebrating increased turnover.

A larger business that does not generate additional cash is not considered successful growth on this dashboard.
