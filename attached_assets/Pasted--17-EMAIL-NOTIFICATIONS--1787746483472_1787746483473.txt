--------------------------------------------------
17. EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Whenever an employee submits an Overtime or Authorised Time Off entry, automatically send an email notification to management.

The email must be sent immediately after the entry is successfully saved.

Do NOT send an email if the save fails.

--------------------------------------------------
OVERTIME EMAIL
--------------------------------------------------

Subject:

JobFlow - Overtime Logged - [Employee Name]

Email body:

An overtime entry has been submitted in JobFlow.

Employee: [Employee Name]
Date: [Date Worked]
Client: [Client Name]
Job: [Job Number / Description]
Start Time: [Start Time]
Finish Time: [Finish Time]

Before 08:00: [Calculated Time]
After 16:00: [Calculated Time]

Total Overtime: [Total Hours and Minutes]

Reason / Notes:
[Notes]

Status: Pending Approval

Include a button/link:

VIEW OVERTIME REQUEST

This must open the relevant overtime entry inside JobFlow.

--------------------------------------------------
TIME OFF EMAIL
--------------------------------------------------

Subject:

JobFlow - Time Off Logged - [Employee Name]

Email body:

An Authorised Time Off entry has been submitted in JobFlow.

Employee: [Employee Name]
Date: [Date]
Start Time: [Start Time]
Finish Time: [Finish Time]

Total Time Off: [Hours and Minutes]

Reason:
[Reason]

Notes:
[Notes]

Status: Pending Approval

Include a button/link:

VIEW TIME OFF REQUEST

This must open the relevant Time Off entry inside JobFlow.

--------------------------------------------------
ADMIN-CREATED TIME OFF
--------------------------------------------------

If an Admin directly creates and approves Authorised Time Off, still send an email notification.

Example:

Subject:
JobFlow - Time Off Authorised - Garth

Body:

Authorised Time Off has been recorded.

Employee: Garth
Date: 26/08/2026
Time: 12:00 - 16:00
Total: 4 hrs
Reason: Gap between jobs
Authorised By: Julien
Status: Approved

--------------------------------------------------
APPROVAL EMAIL
--------------------------------------------------

When an overtime or time-off request is APPROVED, optionally email the employee if the employee has an email address.

Subject:

JobFlow - Overtime Approved

or:

JobFlow - Time Off Approved

Do not require employee email addresses for the system to work.

If the mobile employee does not have an email address, simply update their JobFlow status and do not attempt to email them.

--------------------------------------------------
REJECTION EMAIL
--------------------------------------------------

If a request is rejected, optionally email the employee if an email address exists.

Include:

Type
Date
Time
Total
Rejected By
Rejection Reason

--------------------------------------------------
MANAGEMENT RECIPIENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Create configurable email notification recipients in JobFlow Settings.

Settings > Notifications > Time & Overtime

Fields:

Overtime Notifications:
[Email recipient list]

Time Off Notifications:
[Email recipient list]

Allow multiple email addresses.

Example:

julien@...
service@...
hr@...

Do NOT hard-code the email address into the application.

Admin must be able to add/remove recipients later.

--------------------------------------------------
EMAIL PREFERENCES
--------------------------------------------------

Add notification switches:

[ON] New Overtime Submitted
[ON] New Time Off Submitted
[ON] Overtime Approved
[ON] Time Off Approved
[ON] Overtime Rejected
[ON] Time Off Rejected

Default:

New Overtime Submitted = ON
New Time Off Submitted = ON

The other notifications can be configurable.

--------------------------------------------------
EMAIL FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------

If the overtime/time-off record saves successfully but the email fails:

DO NOT lose or roll back the submitted record.

Save the entry normally.

Log the failed email in the system.

Show Admin a warning such as:

"Time entry saved, but notification email could not be sent."

Allow Admin to resend the notification.

--------------------------------------------------
AUDIT TRAIL
--------------------------------------------------

Also record email activity in the audit trail:

Notification email sent

Notification email resent

Store:
