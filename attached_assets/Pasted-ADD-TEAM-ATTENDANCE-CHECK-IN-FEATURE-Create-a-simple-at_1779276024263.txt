Permissions

Managing Member:
Can view and edit all attendance.

HR / Finance & HR Manager:
Can view all attendance and export reports.

Service Coordinator:
Can view attendance for service teams.

Team Leader / Supervisor:
Can submit attendance for their assigned team only.

Technician:
Can only submit attendance if assigned as a team leader.

Normal worker:
No attendance management access.

Important rules

One attendance record per team per date.

If attendance already exists for today, load it instead of creating a duplicate.

If a team member is not ticked present, they should show as absent or not confirmed depending on the chosen logic.

Preferred logic:
Before submission = Not confirmed
After submission:

Ticked present = Present
Ticked absent = Absent
Unticked = Absent

Add confirmation before submitting if some members are not ticked:

Some team members are not marked present. They will be saved as absent. Continue?

Keep it simple

Do not make this complicated.

HR can see the summaries and reports.
