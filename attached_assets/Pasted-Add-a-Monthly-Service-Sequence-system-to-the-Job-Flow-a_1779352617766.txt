Add a Monthly Service Sequence system to the Job Flow app.

The purpose is to allow the service coordinator to load recurring service jobs in a fixed sequence for each month.

This must not be treated as a normal random calendar list.
Jobs must follow a set order based on:
- week of the month
- day of the week
- job sequence number

Create a new section inside Service called:
Monthly Service Sequence

Each recurring service setup must include:

- customerId
- customerName
- department
- serviceType
- assignedTechnician
- assignedTeam
- serviceFrequency
- serviceWeek
- serviceDay
- jobSequence
- estimatedDuration
- defaultStartTime
- googleMapsLink
- notes
- activeStatus

serviceFrequency options:
- Weekly
- Fortnightly
- Monthly
- Every 2 Months
- Quarterly
- Once-off

serviceWeek options:
- Week 1
- Week 2
- Week 3
- Week 4
- Week 5

serviceDay options:
- Monday
- Tuesday
- Wednesday
- Thursday
- Friday
- Saturday
- Sunday

jobSequence must be a number.
The lower number must appear first on that day.

Example:
Week 1, Monday, Sequence 1
Week 1, Monday, Sequence 2
Week 1, Tuesday, Sequence 1
Week 1, Tuesday, Sequence 2
Week 2, Monday, Sequence 1

Create a Monthly Sequence View that groups jobs like this:

Week 1
  Monday
    1. Customer A
    2. Customer B
    3. Customer C
  Tuesday
    1. Customer D
    2. Customer E

Week 2
  Monday
    1. Customer F
    2. Customer G

The service coordinator must be able to:
- add a customer to the sequence
- edit the sequence
- move a job up or down
- change the service week
- change the service day
- change the assigned technician/team
- deactivate a recurring service
- search by customer
- filter by department
- filter by technician/team

Add drag-and-drop ordering if possible.
If drag-and-drop is too complex, add simple buttons:
Move Up
Move Down

When moving a job up or down, update the jobSequence numbers automatically.

Add a button:
Generate Monthly Jobs

When clicked, the user chooses:
- month
- year
- department
- technician/team optional

The app must generate actual job cards from the Monthly Service Sequence.

Generated job cards must include:
- customer
- department
- service type
- assigned technician/team
- scheduled date
- sequence number
- status: Scheduled
- googleMapsLink
- notes

Important:
Do not create duplicate jobs if the same month has already been generated.
Before generating, check whether jobs already exist for that customer, department, service week, service day, and month.

If jobs already exist, show:
"Some jobs have already been generated for this month. Do you want to skip duplicates?"

Sorting rules:
Always sort jobs by:
1. serviceWeek
2. serviceDay
3. jobSequence

Technician view:
Technicians must see their jobs in the correct sequence order for the day.
Show:
1. Customer name
2. Address
3. Job status
4. Open in Google Maps
5. Start Job
6. Complete Job

Admin calendar:
The calendar must also respect the service sequence.
Jobs for a day must display in sequence order, not alphabetical order.

Add a print/export option:
Monthly Route Sheet

The Monthly Route Sheet must show:
- week
- day
- sequence number
- customer
- address
- technician/team
- department
- Google Maps link if available
