Simplify the JobFlow Sales sidebar menu.

Current Sales menu has too many items:
- Leads
- Quotes
- Accepted Work
- Diary
- Clients
- Follow-ups
- Opportunities
- Reports
- Email Center

Update the Sales menu to show only:

- Leads
- Quotes
- Calendar
- Clients
- Follow-ups
- Reports
- Email Center

MENU CHANGES:

1. Rename "Diary" to "Calendar".

The Calendar must be the same shared JobFlow calendar used by all departments.
It must not be a separate Sales-only diary.

The shared Calendar must show:
- Sales appointments
- Site visits
- Quote follow-ups
- Service jobs
- Operations tasks
- Finance/admin reminders where applicable

All users with permission must be able to view and edit the same calendar.

Calendar filters:
- My items
- All users
- Department
- User
- Item type
- Status
- Date range

Calendar item types:
- Sales appointment
- Site visit
- Quote follow-up
- Service job
- Contract job
- Once-off job
- Installation
- Internal task
- Other

2. Remove "Accepted Work" from the Sales sidebar.

Accepted Work should not be a separate Sales menu item.

When a quote is accepted:
- Convert it to a Job
- The Job must appear under the Jobs/Service workflow
- The Job must appear on the shared Calendar
- The quote must remain visible in Quotes as Accepted / Converted to Job

Sales should be able to see accepted/converted quotes from the Quotes page and Reports, but not as a separate sidebar item.

3. Remove "Opportunities" as a separate menu item.

Opportunities must live inside Leads.

Inside Leads, add filters/tabs:
- All Leads
- New Leads
- Opportunities
- Contacted
- Appointment Booked
- Quote Needed
- Quoted
- Lost
- Converted

An opportunity is simply a lead with a higher chance or active sales potential.
Do not create a separate Opportunities module.

Add field on lead:
- Opportunity: Yes / No

Optional opportunity fields:
- Estimated value
- Expected close date
- Probability %
- Priority
- Next action

4. Clients must be the shared client database.

The Clients page must be a central database that all departments can view and edit according to permissions.

Clients must not be Sales-only.

Client profile must show:
- Client details
- Contacts
- Addresses
- Leads
- Quotes
- Jobs
- Contracts
- Invoices
- Payments/payment status if allowed
- Service history
- Notes
- Attachments
- Photos
- Follow-ups

All users with permission must be able to view and edit client information.

5. Follow-ups can stay separate.

Follow-ups must include:
- Lead follow-ups
- Quote follow-ups
- Client follow-ups
- After-sales follow-ups
- Contract renewal follow-ups
- Price increase reminders

Follow-ups must also appear on the shared Calendar if they have a date/time.

6. Reports can stay.

Reports must include:
- Sales report by date range
- Commission report
- Leads by origination
- Quotes sent
- Quotes accepted
- Quotes lost
- Sales by rep
- Sales by department
- Conversion rate
- Follow-up performance

7. Email Center can stay.

Email Center should allow users to create emails from:
- Lead
- Quote
- Client
- Job
- Follow-up

Emails should open Outlook/default email app using mailto for now.

FINAL SALES MENU ORDER:

Sales
- Leads
- Quotes
- Calendar
- Clients
- Follow-ups
- Reports
- Email Center

IMPORTANT WORKFLOW RULE:

The Sales workflow must stay simple:

Lead → Quote → Job → Invoice

Do not add separate stages/modules that confuse this flow.

Appointments, opportunities, site visits and notes must live inside Leads/Quotes/Calendar.
Accepted work must become Jobs.
Contracts must be linked to Jobs/Clients, not shown as a separate Sales sidebar item.

The goal is to make the Sales menu simple and obvious for reps:
- Leads are prospects
- Quotes are prices sent
- Calendar is where work/appointments happen
- Clients is the shared customer database
- Follow-ups are tasks/reminders
- Reports are for performance and commission
- Email Center opens Outlook emails
