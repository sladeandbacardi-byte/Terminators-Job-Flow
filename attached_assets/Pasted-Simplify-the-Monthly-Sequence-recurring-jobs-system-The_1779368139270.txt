Simplify the Monthly Sequence / recurring jobs system.

The current page is too complicated.

Rename "Monthly Sequence" to:
Contracts

The Job Flow app must have two types of jobs:

1. Once-off Jobs
2. Contract / Recurring Jobs

A once-off job is a single job booked for one date and time.

A contract job is a recurring job that repeats according to a selected frequency, similar to Microsoft Outlook recurring appointments.

Service

- Calendar

- Field Diaries

- Purchase Orders

Remove complicated wording like:
- Monthly Service Sequence

- Sequence number

Replace it with simpler contract scheduling language.

CONTRACT FORM

When adding or editing a contract, show these fields:

Customer
Service Type
Department
Assigned Technician / Team
Frequency
Start Date
End Date optional
Week of Month
Day of Week
Second Day of Week if needed
Start Time
Estimated Duration
Google Maps Link

Frequency options must be:

- Daily
- 2 x a week
- Weekly
- Twice a month
- Monthly
- Every 2 months
- Quarterly
- Every 6 months
- Annually
- Once-off

Rules:

If Frequency is Once-off:
Show:
- Date
- Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

Hide:
- Week of Month
- Day of Week
- Second Day of Week
- End Date

If Frequency is Daily:
Show:
- Start Date
- Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

If Frequency is 2 x a week:
Show:
- Start Date
- First Day of Week
- Second Day of Week
- Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

Example:
Monday and Thursday at 08:00

If Frequency is Weekly:
Show:
- Day of Week
- Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

If Frequency is Twice a month:
Show:
- First Week of Month
- First Day of Week
- First Start Time
- Second Week of Month
- Second Day of Week
- Second Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

Example:
1st Monday at 08:00 and 3rd Monday at 08:00

If Frequency is Monthly:
Show:
- Week of Month
- Day of Week
- Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

Example:
1st week, Monday, 08:00

If Frequency is Every 2 months:
Show the same fields as Monthly, but repeat every 2 months.

If Frequency is Quarterly:
Show the same fields as Monthly, but repeat every 3 months.

If Frequency is Every 6 months:
Show the same fields as Monthly, but repeat every 6 months.

If Frequency is Annually:
Show:
- Month
- Week of Month
- Day of Week
- Start Time
- Estimated Duration
- Assigned Technician / Team

Week of Month options:

- 1st week
- 2nd week
- 3rd week
- 4th week
- Last week

Day of Week options:

- Monday
- Tuesday
- Wednesday
- Thursday
- Friday
- Saturday
- Sunday
