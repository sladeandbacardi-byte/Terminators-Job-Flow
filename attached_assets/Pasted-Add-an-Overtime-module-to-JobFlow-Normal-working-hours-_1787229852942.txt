Add an Overtime module to JobFlow.

Normal working hours are 08:00 to 16:00. Only time worked before 08:00 and after 16:00 qualifies as overtime.

Create a My Overtime page for employees and an Overtime Approval page for Admin/Management.

Each overtime entry must contain:

Employee, automatically set to the logged-in user
Date worked
Client
Job/job card where applicable
Start time
Finish time
Notes/reason
Automatically calculated overtime minutes
Status: Pending, Approved or Rejected
Approved By
Approval timestamp

Use proper mobile-friendly time input controls. Users must not manually enter the overtime total.

Calculation logic:

Any time between 08:00 and 16:00 is normal working time and must NOT count as overtime.
Time before 08:00 counts as overtime.
Time after 16:00 counts as overtime.
If a shift spans both periods, add the before-08:00 portion and the after-16:00 portion.

Examples:

06:30–08:00 = 1 hr 30 min overtime
07:00–09:00 = 1 hr overtime
15:00–17:30 = 1 hr 30 min overtime
07:30–17:00 = 1 hr 30 min overtime
08:00–16:00 = 0 overtime
09:00–15:00 = 0 overtime
06:30–17:30 = 3 hrs overtime

If the calculated overtime is zero, prevent submission and show: “No overtime detected. Normal working hours are 08:00 to 16:00.”

Store overtime internally in minutes, not decimal hours, but display totals as hours and minutes.

Add a Log Overtime action from a Job Card. When opened from a Job Card, automatically populate the employee, client, job and date.

Employees can view their own overtime history and totals but cannot approve overtime.

Admin/Management can view all overtime, filter by employee/date/status, approve or reject individual entries, select multiple entries and approve them together, and see total pending/approved overtime hours.

Record an audit trail showing who submitted, approved, rejected or edited each entry.

Once approved, an employee cannot edit the entry unless an administrator reopens it.

Make the entire interface responsive and easy to use on a mobile phone.
