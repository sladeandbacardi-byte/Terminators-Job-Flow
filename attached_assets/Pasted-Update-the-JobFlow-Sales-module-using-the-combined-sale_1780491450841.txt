OVERTIME WARNING:
When creating a quote, ask:
"Will this service need to be done after hours or during overtime?"

If Yes, show:
"Remember to add overtime / after-hours rate to the quote."

OUTRIGHT PURCHASE WARNING:
If Quote Type = Outright Purchase, show:
"Remember to add installation cost if installation is required."

If no installation cost is added, show before sending:
"This quote has no installation cost. Confirm before sending."

Sales reps can:

- Add leads

- Schedule appointments
- Create quotes
- Add follow-up notes
- Upload site photos
- Upload inspection notes
- Mark appointments completed
- Request quote approval if needed

- Cost prices
- Margins
- Profit reports
- Full debtor ledger
- Business financial reports

DESIGN:
Keep the Sales module clean and simple.

Use top menu:
Sales Dashboard | Leads | Sales Diary | Quotes | Follow-ups | Contracts Pending | Client Registration | Clients | Pricing Library | Commission Reports | Sales Reports

Make the Sales Diary look similar to an Outlook calendar.

Make the client profile a central “client file” where sales can quickly see:
- Services
- Contracts
- Prices
- Frequencies
- Increase month
- Service team
- Quotes
- Jobs
- Invoices
- Notes
- Photos
- Attachments

The goal is to make quoting quicker, prevent leads and quotes from being forgotten, make client history visible, make accepted quotes flow into registration/contracts/scheduling, and make monthly commission reporting much easier.
