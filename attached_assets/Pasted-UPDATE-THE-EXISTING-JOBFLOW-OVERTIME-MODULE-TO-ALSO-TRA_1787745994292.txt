UPDATE THE EXISTING JOBFLOW OVERTIME MODULE TO ALSO TRACK AUTHORISED TIME OFF.

Do not create a separate disconnected application.

Rename the employee-facing area to:

MY TIME

Under My Time provide two actions:

+ LOG OVERTIME
+ LOG TIME OFF

The Admin area can be called:

TIME & OVERTIME

The purpose is to record both:
1. Work performed outside normal working hours
2. Periods during normal working hours where management has authorised an employee not to work

NORMAL WORKING HOURS

08:00 to 16:00

--------------------------------------------------
1. AUTHORISED TIME OFF
--------------------------------------------------

Add a new type:

AUTHORISED TIME OFF

This is NOT annual leave, sick leave or unpaid leave.

It is used when Terminators allows an employee to stop working during part of the normal work day.

Example:

Employee completes their work at 12:00.
Their next client appointment is at 16:00.
Management allows them to go home between jobs.

Record:

Time Off Start: 12:00
Time Off Finish: 16:00
Total Time Off: 4 hrs

The employee then attends the next job at 16:00.

If they work:

16:00 - 17:00

the existing overtime system records:

Overtime: 1 hr

The employee's day would therefore show:

08:00 - 12:00     Working
12:00 - 16:00     Authorised Time Off
16:00 - 17:00     Working / Overtime

3. TIME OFF VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Authorised Time Off should normally only apply inside normal working hours:

08:00 - 16:00

Only count the portion that falls between 08:00 and 16:00.

Examples:

12:00 - 16:00
= 4 hrs authorised time off

13:30 - 16:00
= 2 hrs 30 min

08:00 - 10:00
= 2 hrs

07:00 - 10:00
= 2 hrs Time Off
Only 08:00 - 10:00 falls inside normal working hours.

15:00 - 17:00
= 1 hr Time Off
Only 15:00 - 16:00 falls inside normal working hours.

If there is no portion between 08:00 and 16:00:
Do not allow Time Off to be submitted.

Show:

"No normal working time exists in this period."

--------------------------------------------------
5. APPROVAL
--------------------------------------------------

Employees may submit Time Off.

Time Off must be approved by Admin/Management.

However, also allow Admin/Management to create Time Off directly for an employee.

If an Admin creates the Time Off entry:
- Allow it to be saved immediately as Approved
- Record which Admin authorised it

Employees cannot approve their own Time Off.

Once approved, employees cannot edit it.

--------------------------------------------------
12. IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE FROM LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT treat Authorised Time Off as:

- Annual Leave
- Sick Leave
- Unpaid Leave
- Absence

Authorised Time Off means:

"Management has authorised the employee not to work for a specified period during their normal working day."

It must not reduce an employee's leave balance.

Leave can be developed as a separate HR module later.

--------------------------------------------------
13. PREVENT OVERLAPPING ENTRIES
--------------------------------------------------

Check for conflicting Time Off entries.

Example:

Existing:
12:00 - 16:00 Time Off

Do not allow another:
13:00 - 15:00 Time Off

Warn:

"This employee already has a Time Off entry covering this period."

Also check against scheduled JobFlow jobs.

If an employee tries to log:

Time Off:
12:00 - 16:00

but JobFlow has a scheduled job:
14:00 - 15:00

display a warning:

"This employee has a scheduled job during the selected Time Off period."

Show the conflicting job details.

Admin can then:
- Cancel
- Adjust the Time Off period
- Override with a reason

Record any Admin override in the audit trail.
