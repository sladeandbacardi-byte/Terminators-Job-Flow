RESTRUCTURE JOB FLOW MENU AND BUSINESS PROCESS

I want to simplify the left-hand sidebar and make the app follow the actual business process flow.

The sidebar is currently too busy.

Change it to a simpler linear menu structure with main sections.

Use these main sidebar sections:

DASHBOARD
Dashboard
SALES

Sales is for bringing in and converting new work.

Under Sales show:

Leads
Quotes
Clients
Rental Contracts
Email Center

Sales process:
A potential client becomes a lead.
Any staff member with permission can create a lead.
The lead can be assigned to a sales rep.
The sales rep contacts the client, adds notes, clarifies needs and creates a quote.
If the quote is accepted, it must convert to a job.

SERVICE

Service is for work that must be planned, scheduled and completed.

Under Service show:

Jobs
Calendar
Field Diaries
Stock Management
Stock Manager Pro
Suppliers
Purchase Orders

Service process:
When a quote is accepted, create a linked job.
The job moves from Sales to Service.
The job appears in the Service > Jobs section.
Service coordinators assign the worker/team, date, time, department and job details.
Technicians complete the work.
Technicians submit field diaries, notes, photos and signatures if needed.
Completed jobs can then be sent to Finance for invoicing.

FINANCE

Finance is for invoicing, money owing to us, money we owe, and financial exports.

Under Finance show:

Invoices
Debtors
Creditors
Sage Export
Backup & Restore
Reports

Finance process:
When a job is completed and ready to bill, it can be converted to an invoice.
The invoice must keep links to:

Original lead if available
Quote document number
Job document number
Client
Service details

Finance users can view invoices, debtors, creditors, exports and backup tools.

OPERATIONS

Operations is for staff, attendance, fleet and business support.

Under Operations show:

Staff
Team Attendance
Teams
Fleet Dashboard
Maintenance
Report Issue
Log KMs
Vehicle Inspection
Fuel Fill-up
Testing Checklist

Operations process:
Operations manages people, vehicles, attendance and general support.
Team leaders can submit daily team attendance.
HR/Admin can view attendance by date, team, department and supervisor.
Fleet records include vehicle checks, issues, kilometres, fuel and maintenance.

ADMIN

Admin is for system settings and management only.

Under Admin show:

Users & Roles
Permissions
Settings
System Logs
Backup & Restore

Only Managing Member/Admin users should see full Admin.

BUSINESS WORKFLOW

Create a clear document flow:

Lead
↓
Quote
↓
Job
↓
Field Diary / Completion
↓
Invoice
↓
Finance / Debtors / Sage Export

Document links:
Lead can link to Quote.
Quote can link to Job.
Job can link to Field Diary.
Job can link to Invoice.
Invoice must show linked Job and Quote document numbers.

Example:
Lead: LEAD-2026-0001
Quote: QT-2026-0004
Job: JOB-2026-0021
Field Diary: FD-2026-0034
Invoice: INV-2026-0015

On each document detail page, show linked documents.

Example on Job:
Linked Quote: QT-2026-0004
Linked Invoice: INV-2026-0015
Field Diary: FD-2026-0034

Example on Invoice:
Linked Job: JOB-2026-0021
Linked Quote: QT-2026-0004
