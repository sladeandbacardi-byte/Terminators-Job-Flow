CONCEPT

A once-off job can be converted into a contract.

Add a button on completed or approved jobs:

Convert to Contract

When clicked, open a contract creation form and pre-fill details from the job:

- client
- address
- service type
- department
- assigned team if available
- notes
- Google Maps link if available

The user must then complete the contract scheduling details.

SCHEDULING RULES

If frequency is Once-off:
Show date and time only.

If frequency is Daily:
Show start date, time, estimated duration, assigned team.

If frequency is 2 x a week:
Show first day of week, second day of week, time, estimated duration, assigned team.

If frequency is Weekly:
Show day of week, time, estimated duration, assigned team.

If frequency is Twice a month:
Show:
- first week of month
- first day of week
- first time
- second week of month
- second day of week
- second time

If frequency is Monthly, Every 2 months, Quarterly, Every 6 months:
Show:
- week of month
- day of week
- time
- estimated duration
- assigned team
- route order

If frequency is Annually:
Show:
- month
- week of month
- day of week
- time
- estimated duration
- assigned team
- route order

SCHEDULE VIEW

Inside Contracts, create a tab called Schedule View.

This is only a view of contract schedules.
Users should not create separate schedule entries here.

The Schedule View must display active contracts grouped by:

1. Week of Month
2. Day of Week
3. Time
4. Route Order

Example:

Week 1
  Monday
    08:00 - 1. Volkswagen SA - Washroom Contract
    09:00 - 2. Client B - Sanitary Bins

  Tuesday
    08:00 - 1. Client C - Dustmats

Each row must show:

- route order
- time
- client
- service type
- frequency
- assigned team
- contract status
- edit contract button

When the user clicks Edit, open the actual contract record.

CALENDAR

The Calendar must display recurring jobs based on active contract scheduling.

The Calendar must read from contract data.

Do not require users to manually create schedule entries separately.

DAILY DIARIES

Daily Diaries must also read from active contract schedules.

The diary must show jobs for the selected date in time and route order.

JOBS

Once-off jobs stay in the Jobs section.

Contract jobs come from active contracts.

The user can convert a once-off job into a contract using Convert to Contract.

IMPORTANT

Do not duplicate scheduling data between a separate Service Scheduling table and the Contract table.

The Contract is the source of truth.

The Schedule View is only a visual display of the contracts by week, day, time, and route order.
