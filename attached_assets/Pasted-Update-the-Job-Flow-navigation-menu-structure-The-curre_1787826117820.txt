CALENDAR / DIARY RULE

Remove Daily Diaries from the Service menu.

Daily Diaries are no longer a separate menu item.

The Calendar is the diary.

The Calendar must show:
- once-off jobs
- contract jobs
- technician/team daily diary view
- day/week/month views
- Outlook-style diary/calendar layout

If users need a daily diary, they must use the Calendar in Day view with filters.

Calendar filters must allow:
- Date
- Technician
- Team
- Department
- Service Type
- Client
- Area/Suburb
- Status
- Contract Jobs / Once-off Jobs

1. Contract Jobs

This shows job occurrences generated from active contracts.

Contract Jobs must include:
- client
- contract number
- service type
- department
- scheduled date
- scheduled time
- route sequence
- assigned technician/team
- status
- invoice status
- open job
- open contract
- open client

2. Once-off Jobs

This shows once-off service jobs.

Once-off Jobs must include:
- client
- job title
- service type
- department
- date
- time

- status
- invoice status
- open job
- open client

- Contract List

- Contracts Ending
