Add an OVERTIME module to Terminators JobFlow.

PURPOSE
Allow employees to log overtime worked against a client/job, automatically calculate valid overtime hours, submit it for approval, and allow Admin/Management to approve or reject it.

NORMAL WORKING HOURS
Normal working hours are:

08:00 to 16:00

Only time worked:
- BEFORE 08:00
- AFTER 16:00

counts as overtime.

Time worked between 08:00 and 16:00 must NEVER count as overtime.

3. OVERTIME CALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------

Calculate overtime automatically.

Normal working period:
08:00 up to 16:00

OVERTIME BEFORE WORK:
Any portion of the work period before 08:00.

OVERTIME AFTER WORK:
Any portion of the work period after 16:00.

TOTAL OVERTIME:
before-08:00 overtime + after-16:00 overtime

DO NOT calculate the entire shift as overtime if the shift overlaps normal working hours.

Examples:

06:30 - 08:00
= 1 hour 30 minutes overtime

07:00 - 09:00
= 1 hour overtime

07:30 - 08:30
= 30 minutes overtime

08:00 - 16:00
= 0 overtime

09:00 - 15:00
= 0 overtime

15:00 - 17:30
= 1 hour 30 minutes overtime

16:00 - 17:00
= 1 hour overtime

07:30 - 17:00
= 1 hour 30 minutes overtime

06:30 - 17:30
= 3 hours overtime:
06:30-08:00 = 1 hour 30 minutes
16:00-17:30 = 1 hour 30 minutes

--------------------------------------------------
4. IMPORTANT STORAGE RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Store overtime internally as MINUTES.

Examples:

30 minutes = 30
1 hour = 60
1 hour 30 minutes = 90
2 hours 15 minutes = 135

Do NOT store overtime as values such as:
1.30
1.5
1.75

Display overtime to users as:

30 min
1 hr
1 hr 30 min
2 hrs 15 min

This is important for accurate payroll calculations.

--------------------------------------------------
5. VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Only allow valid times.

Reject invalid values such as:
25:00
12:75
letters/text
blank start time
blank finish time

Finish time must logically follow start time.

If calculated overtime = 0, do not allow the employee to submit the overtime request.

Show:

"No overtime detected. Normal working hours are 08:00 to 16:00."

Prevent duplicate entries where the same employee has the same:
- Date
- Start time
- Finish time
- Job

Warn the user before saving a possible duplicate.

22. IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------

Do not build Overtime as a standalone disconnected feature.

It must use the existing JobFlow database and link overtime records to:

EMPLOYEE
   ↓
CLIENT
   ↓
JOB
   ↓
OVERTIME
   ↓
APPROVAL
   ↓
REPORT / PAYROLL

An employee's overtime must therefore also be visible from:
- Employee Profile
- Job history where linked to a job
- Client history where linked to a client

Keep all information interconnected with the existing JobFlow system.
